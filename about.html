Our Story

Built for African
investors, by African analysts

We believe every person on this continent deserves access to the same quality of financial analysis that institutional investors take for granted.

What we do

The African Oracle is a free stock analysis and screening tool covering every company listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) in Kenya and the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE) in South Africa — over 200 companies combined.

We compile and present financial data including earnings per share, price-to-earnings ratios, dividend yields, profit margins, debt levels and proprietary quality scores — all updated daily — in a clean, easy-to-understand format. Our goal is to give individual investors the analytical foundation they need to make educated investment decisions.

Who we are

We are independent financial data analysts with a focus on African capital markets. We are not fund managers, licensed financial advisors, or stockbrokers. We are researchers and analysts who are passionate about making African stock market data accessible to everyone — from first-time investors to seasoned professionals.

Everything on this platform is built from publicly available information: company annual reports, exchange filings, and verified financial databases. We do not have privileged access to company information, and we do not trade on or profit from the analysis we publish.

Why we built this

African retail investors have historically been underserved by financial data platforms. Most global tools focus on US and European markets. The few African-focused tools that exist are either expensive, require account registration, or present data in formats that are not accessible to everyday investors.

We wanted to change that. The African Oracle is completely free, requires no registration, and presents data in plain English with explanations anyone can understand — regardless of their financial background. Our glossary page explains every metric we use in simple terms with real examples from NSE and JSE companies.

How our data works

Our data pipeline combines live market data with carefully researched historical fundamentals:

  • NSE Kenya prices — fetched daily from a live market data API
  • JSE South Africa prices — fetched daily via Google Finance
  • Historical EPS and margins — sourced from company annual reports (FY2020 to FY2024/25)
  • Quality scores (Moat, Financial Strength, Predictability) — calculated using a rules-based model built on sector analysis, margin trends, and earnings consistency
  • Dividend yields — calculated from annual dividend per share data divided by current market price

Data is refreshed daily and cached for fast delivery. Our quality scores are algorithmic and based on publicly available information — they are not investment recommendations.

What we are not

We want to be clear about our limitations:

  • We are not licensed financial advisors — nothing on this site is financial advice
  • We are not affiliated with the NSE or JSE — we are an independent data platform
  • We are not a brokerage — we do not execute trades or hold client funds
  • We do not guarantee the accuracy of all data — always verify important figures with official company filings

Our signals (Buy, Watch, Avoid) are algorithmic outputs based on quantitative metrics. They do not account for qualitative factors, macroeconomic conditions, your personal financial situation, or your risk tolerance. Always consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.

Our mission

To democratise access to quality financial analysis across Africa — one stock at a time. We believe an informed investor is a better investor, and that African capital markets will grow stronger as more individual investors participate with confidence and knowledge.

If you have feedback, data corrections, or suggestions, we would love to hear from you via our contact page.
