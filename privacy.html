Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who we are

The African Oracle (africanoracle.info) is a free stock analysis tool covering companies listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) in Kenya and the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE) in South Africa. We compile publicly available financial data and present it in an accessible format to help investors make informed decisions.

We are independent analysts. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the NSE, JSE, or any listed company.

2. What data we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use this service. We do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.

When you visit the site, the following may be collected automatically by third-party services we use:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Referring website (where you came from)

3. Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history across sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites.

You may opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

We also link to third-party brokerage platforms (EasyEquities, SBG Securities, NCBA Securities, Absa Securities, Satrix, Standard Bank OST, Zidii). These sites have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

4. How we use data

Any data collected via analytics is used solely to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We do not sell, trade, or rent your data to third parties. We do not use your data for marketing purposes.

5. Stock data sources

Financial data displayed on The African Oracle is sourced from publicly available records including:

  • NSE Kenya company annual reports and public disclosures
  • JSE South Africa company annual reports and public disclosures
  • Google Finance (via GoogleFinance function in Google Sheets)
  • Nairobi Stock Exchange API via RapidAPI
  • StockAnalysis.com and other public financial databases

We make every effort to ensure accuracy but cannot guarantee that all data is complete or up to date. Data is for informational purposes only.

6. Investment disclaimer

All information on The African Oracle is for educational and informational purposes only. Nothing on this site constitutes financial advice, investment advice, trading advice, or any other type of advice. You should not treat any of the site's content as financial advice.

Always conduct your own research and consult a qualified, regulated financial advisor before making any investment decisions. Past performance of a stock is not indicative of future results.

7. Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is typically no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. For data held by Google (analytics, advertising), you can manage your preferences through Google's account settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please use our contact page.
