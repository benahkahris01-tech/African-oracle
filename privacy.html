Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who we are

The African Oracle (africanoracle.info) is a free stock analysis tool covering companies listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) in Kenya and the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE) in South Africa. We compile publicly available financial data and present it in an accessible format to help investors make informed decisions.

We are independent analysts. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the NSE, JSE, or any listed company.

2. What data we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use this service. We do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.

When you visit the site, the following may be collected automatically by third-party services we use:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country and city level)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, or tablet)
  • Referring website (where you came from before visiting this site)

3. Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history across websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

We also link to third-party brokerage platforms including Zidii Trader, SBG Securities, NCBA Securities, EasyEquities, Standard Bank Online Share Trading, and Satrix. These sites have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review before opening any account.

4. How we use data

Any data collected via analytics tools is used solely to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not build personal profiles of individual users.

5. Stock data sources

Financial data displayed on The African Oracle is sourced from publicly available records including:

  • NSE Kenya company annual reports and public regulatory disclosures
  • JSE South Africa company annual reports and public regulatory disclosures
  • Google Finance (via the GoogleFinance function)
  • Nairobi Stock Exchange data API via RapidAPI
  • StockAnalysis.com and other public financial databases

We make every reasonable effort to ensure accuracy but cannot guarantee that all data is complete or current. Data is provided for informational purposes only. Always verify important figures with official company filings.

6. Investment disclaimer

All information on The African Oracle is for educational and informational purposes only. Nothing on this site constitutes financial advice, investment advice, trading advice, or any other type of professional advice. You should not treat any of the site's content as financial advice.

Always conduct your own independent research and consult a qualified, regulated financial advisor — registered with the Capital Markets Authority (CMA) of Kenya or the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA) of South Africa — before making any investment decisions. Past performance of a stock is not indicative of future results.

7. Your rights

Since we do not directly collect personal data, there is typically no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. For data held by Google through analytics or advertising, you can manage your preferences through Google's Account Settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as the site evolves or as legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please contact us via our contact page. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 2 business days.
