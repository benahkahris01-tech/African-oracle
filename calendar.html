How this calendar works

NSE and JSE do not publish structured machine-readable earnings date feeds. Our team monitors company announcements, exchange notices, and historical reporting patterns to estimate upcoming results dates. Dates marked Confirmed have been verified from an official company announcement. Dates marked Estimated are our best estimate based on historical patterns and should be treated as approximate.

If you spot an incorrect or missing date, please let us know — we update the calendar within 24 hours.

Results dates are for informational purposes only. Not financial advice. Always verify official dates with company announcements on the NSE website or JSE website.
